Privacy Policy

Overview

Sudoku Supreme is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information to third parties. All data is stored locally on your device and optionally synced to your personal iCloud account.

Data We Store

The app stores the following data solely to provide core functionality: • Your games: the puzzle, your numbers and pencil marks, misses, time, hints used, and a log of your moves (used for the game review) • Completed puzzle records (level, time, misses, score) and wins per level • Technique stats (how quickly you spot each solving technique) • App preferences (palette, keypad side, hint setting) This data stays on your device and in your personal iCloud account. We have no access to it.

Camera

The Scan Puzzle feature uses your device camera solely to photograph a Sudoku puzzle for recognition. Photos are processed entirely on-device using Apple's Vision framework. No images are stored, transmitted, or shared.

iCloud Sync

If you are signed into iCloud, your games, history, and stats sync across your devices using Apple's iCloud services (iCloud Key-Value Storage and your private CloudKit database). No sign-in to us is needed or possible. This data lives in your personal iCloud account, is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy (apple.com/privacy), and is not visible to us. If you are not signed into iCloud, everything stays on your device.

Hints with Apple Intelligence

On devices with Apple Intelligence, hint text can be reworded by Apple's on-device language model. This happens entirely on your device; nothing is sent to us or to any server.

Analytics & Tracking

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking frameworks. The app does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

Sudoku Supreme does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. As no personal data is collected from any users, the app is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in an updated version of the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the App Store support link on our app page.
