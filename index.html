Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Please open an issue on GitHub. We read everything.

How do I sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Sign into the same iCloud account on both devices. Games, history and stats sync automatically; there's nothing to set up and no account to create. Open the app on the other device and your games are in the Library.

Can I scan a puzzle I've already started on paper?

Yes. Tap the camera button at the top of the home screen, line the grid up in the square, and take the photo. On the check screen, printed numbers are bold and your own numbers are lighter. Tap a cell to fix a number, or hold it to switch between printed and yours, then tap Solve and play.

Do hints cost points?

By default each hint level costs a few points, and showing the answer costs the same as a miss. Turn on Free hints in Settings if you'd rather they never affect your score.

Does the app collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your games stay on your device and in your own iCloud. See the privacy policy for details.
